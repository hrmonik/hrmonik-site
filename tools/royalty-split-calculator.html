How splits actually work

Every song has two copyrights: the composition (the writing — melody, lyrics, chords) and the master (the recording itself). Writers split the composition; artists, producers and whoever funded the recording split the master. Run this calculator once for each side — the percentages are usually different.

Ground rules that save friendships

  • Agree on splits in the session, the day the song is made — before anyone knows if it's a hit.
  • Put it in writing (even a group text counts as evidence; a split sheet is better).
  • Producer points on the master typically run 2–5% of the artist's share, or a flat fee plus points.
  • If everyone contributed and nobody can agree, equal splits are the industry-standard default.

FAQ

Do splits have to be equal?
No — they're whatever the collaborators agree to. Equal splits are just the default when nothing is negotiated.

Is this calculator legal advice?
No. It does the arithmetic; the agreement between collaborators is the legal part. For serious money, get a split sheet signed and talk to a music attorney.
