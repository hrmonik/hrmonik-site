Support

Lojik · Logic & Chess Puzzles · Last updated September 1, 2026

Need help with Lojik? Email info@hrmonik.com. We read every message and usually reply within one or two business days.

What to include

To help us resolve things quickly, please tell us your device model, your iOS version, and the puzzle or screen where the problem happened. A screenshot is always welcome.

Trial and pricing

Lojik gives you seven days of full access when you first open it. After that, continuing costs $8.99 a month, $39.99 a year, or $99.99 once for lifetime access. Accessibility settings and Comfort mode are free and stay free, whether or not you ever pay.

The trial runs once per device. Deleting Lojik and installing it again will not start it over, because the first launch date is kept in the device keychain and survives deletion. That entry holds only the date. It says nothing about you and never leaves your device.

Purchases and restoring

Purchases are handled by Apple. If you have already paid and your access is not showing up, open Lojik, go to Settings, and tap Restore Purchases. Refunds and billing questions are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your progress

Puzzle progress, settings, ratings, and Comfort preferences are stored only on your own device. Deleting the app removes them, and they cannot be recovered afterwards.

Bugs and feature requests

Found something broken, or have an idea for a puzzle type you would like to see? Send it to info@hrmonik.com. Feature requests shape what gets built next.

Privacy

Lojik collects no personal data. Full details are in the privacy policy.
