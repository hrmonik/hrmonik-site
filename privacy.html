Lojik · Privacy

Privacy Policy

Lojik · Logic & Chess Puzzles · Last updated September 1, 2026

Lojik does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It requires no account and no sign-in, and asks for no personal information. The puzzles run offline. The only time Lojik needs a connection is when you start or restore a purchase, and Apple handles that part.

What we don't do

We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers. We do not sell or share any information, because we do not collect any. There are no cookies and no hidden data collection of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings, ratings, and Comfort preferences are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

The trial timestamp

One thing does survive deleting the app. Lojik saves a single timestamp in your device keychain marking when it was first opened. That is deliberate: it is what keeps the seven day trial from being restarted by deleting and reinstalling. The entry holds nothing but that date. It contains no information about you and it never leaves your device.

Trial and purchases

Lojik opens with seven days of full access. After that, continuing costs $8.99 a month, $39.99 a year, or $99.99 once for lifetime access. Accessibility settings and Comfort mode are free and stay free, with no trial and no purchase involved.

Every transaction is handled by Apple through the App Store under Apple's own privacy terms. We never see your payment details.

If you contact us

If you email us feedback or a support request, we use your message solely to reply to you. We do not add you to any list or use your address for anything else.

Children

Lojik is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
