Privacy Policy

Lojik — Logic & Chess Puzzles · Last updated August 31, 2026

Lojik does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app works entirely offline. It requires no account and no sign-in, and asks for no personal information.

What we don't do

We do not use analytics, advertising, or third-party trackers. We do not sell or share any information, because we do not collect any. There are no cookies and no hidden data collection of any kind.

What stays on your device

Your puzzle progress, settings, ratings, and Comfort preferences are stored only on your own device. They are never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app removes them.

Purchases

If you buy Lojik Plus, the transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store and Apple's own privacy terms. We never see your payment details.

If you contact us

If you email us feedback or a support request, we use your message solely to reply to you. We do not add you to any list or use your address for anything else.

Children

Lojik is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.
